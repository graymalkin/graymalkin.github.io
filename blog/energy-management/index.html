A month in…

It’s been an unseasonably mild October/November so far this year, we’ve had the solar setup for a little over month and been able to export for about 3 weeks. In that time we’ve had 5 Axle “grid events”, which have netted us about £30 in credit plus a £25 sign-up bonus ([referral link][axle] if anyone is interested). We are still on the 60 A fuse at the time of writing, UKPN are here today and tomorrow to do the upgrade. Being on just 60 A hasn’t been a problem, although I have been cognisant of it…

Octopus saving sessions, happy hours, and a 60A fuse

Octopus do saving sessions where they credit you for using less energy, and that’s calculated as net energy use so exports are basically paid at a higher rate (the export rate plus the load shifting credits). Credit varies, but it’s between 72 and 144 “octopoints” per kWh, which works out at 9p to 18p per kWh on top of the export rate. Unsurprisingly this coincides with high export payments as well, so we can get up to 50p/kWh exported. That’s great! They call these hours of load shifting saving sessions. We get notified by email, click ‘yes’ in the email (active assent is required by the grid operator), and PredBat picks up the higher export rate via the Octopus HACS addon. All works great.

Another reward from these saving sessions is credit towards a “happy hour”. Each saving session where you successfully load shift gets you a token, two (ish) tokens gets you 1 hr of free energy on a Sunday at a timeslot of your choosing. This is where I’ve had to be thoughtful about the main fuse. With the battery charging at 8 kW, and doing all laundry/dishwashing at the same time, our total load peaks at around 12.5 kW. On a 240v supply that’s 52A which is getting fairly close to our 60A maximum. I definitely shouldn’t boil the kettle and/or start baking at the same time!

That will all be better with a 100A fuse, so hopefully by Christmas we can really juice the grid in those happy hours. Although Octopus have a fair use limit of 15 kWh per happy hour, so we’ll stay within that. 15 kWh at our night rate of 14p/kWh is still £2.10, so happy days for us regardless!
